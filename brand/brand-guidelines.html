The brand

Novafer joins nova, a new star, with fer, French for iron. The spark in the logo is that star landing on the iron: the moment a raw bar becomes a precise part. Everything the brand says and shows should feel like the work itself: exact, dependable, and plainly stated.

Précision

Exact to the millimetre

Dimensions, quantities and prices are stated exactly. A Novafer document never rounds away a millime.

Fiabilité

Delivered as promised

Delivery dates, certificates and stock figures are commitments, so they appear only when they are true.

Clarté

Readable at a glance

Short sentences, real numbers, one idea per line. A foreman and an accountant both understand it.

Proximité

A partner nearby

Named contacts, direct phone lines, and a quick answer when a part is not right.

Logo

The logo is a monogram and a wordmark. The monogram is an N built from two steel stems and a diagonal; the right stem stops short and its top becomes the ember spark. The wordmark is set in Archivo Bold and supplied as outlines; never retype it.

Primary logoDefault on white and light grounds.
ReversedOn Night (#13204F) and dark photography.
StackedSquare formats: signage, social avatars, stamps.
CompactWithout “Industries”, for small or tight spaces.
One colourFax, engraving, laser marking, single-colour print.
App iconFavicon, ERP, mobile home screen.
  1. 1

    48-unit grid. The icon is a 48 × 48 square with an 11-unit corner radius. Every stem is 6 units wide.

  2. 2

    Two stems and a diagonal. Like a structural profile: the left stem runs full height, the diagonal joins the top left to the bottom right.

  3. 3

    The spark. A four-point star with concave sides, 14.4 units across, centred where the right stem would end. It is always Ember on colour, or the same colour as the N in one-colour use.

  4. 4

    Optical centring. N and spark are shifted as one group (−2.1, +2.9) so the mass sits in the middle of the square.

Iconography

Line icons from the open-source Lucide set: 24-unit grid, 1.6 stroke, round caps. Shown at 16 or 20 px next to a label; an icon alone always has an accessible name.

Voice & writing

Novafer writes like a good workshop foreman talks: direct, precise and polite. French is the working language; product names and figures stay exact in every language.

Say the fact

Lead with the number, the date or the reference. « 20 factures échues » before any comment about them.

Name the action

Buttons say what happens: « Émettre la facture », « Enregistrer un règlement », never « OK » or « Valider » alone.

Explain the fix

An error says what is wrong and what to do next, without apologies: « Le montant dépasse le reste à payer (4 508,486 TND). »

Write this
  • Livraison sous 3 semaines après accord.A commitment with a number.
  • Seule une facture en brouillon peut être modifiée.States the rule and the way out.
  • Facture FAC-2026-0169 émise.Confirmation names the document.
Not this
  • Livraison ultra-rapide garantie !!Hype, no number.
  • Oups, une erreur est survenue.Vague and too cute for a legal document.
  • Opération effectuée avec succès.Says nothing about what happened.

Glossary

Quote. Numbered DEV-YYYY-NNNN, valid 30 days.
Facture
Invoice. Numbered FAC-YYYY-NNNN at issue only; drafts have no number.
Réclamation
Client complaint, REC-YYYY-NNNN, linked to its invoice and product.
Encours
Outstanding receivables, all taxes included.

Payment due date.
FODEC
1 % industrial competitiveness levy, included in the VAT base.
Timbre fiscal
Fixed stamp duty on each invoice (1,000 TND).
Matricule fiscal
Tax identifier, printed for both parties on every invoice.
